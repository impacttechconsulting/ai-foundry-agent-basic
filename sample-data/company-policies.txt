# Company Policies

## Remote Work Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Updated: September 15, 2025

### Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements to ensure productivity, communication, and work-life balance for all employees.

### Eligibility
Remote work arrangements are available to employees who:
- Have completed their 90-day probationary period
- Demonstrate consistent performance and reliability
- Have a suitable home office environment
- Obtain approval from their direct supervisor

### Guidelines
1. Employees must maintain the same working hours as their in-office schedule unless otherwise agreed upon.
2. All remote employees must have a secure internet connection and appropriate technology.
3. Regular check-ins with supervisors are required at least twice weekly.
4. All company data must be handled according to our security protocols.
5. Employees working remotely are expected to be available during business hours via email, phone, or video conference.

### Communication Expectations
- Respond to emails within 2 hours during business hours
- Join all scheduled meetings virtually
- Use collaboration tools as required by your department
- Maintain a professional environment during video calls

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Behavior
- Respect all colleagues regardless of position, background, or personal characteristics
- Maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information
- Demonstrate integrity in all business dealings
- Report any violations of company policy immediately

### Technology Use
- Company-provided devices are to be used for business purposes
- Personal use should be minimal and not interfere with productivity
- All software must be properly licensed
- Internet usage should align with business needs
